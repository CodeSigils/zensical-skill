# Bounded article-quality review

Use this reference only when the user asks for article-quality review or an
authorized broader revision. It does not establish a universal blog voice or
SEO formula; follow the target repository's editorial guidance.

Review the page and its rendered route where available. Check:

- reader promise and recognizable opening;
- natural flow, transitions, and proportionate structure;
- visible recommendation, interpretation, lesson, or uncertainty;
- useful examples, tips, experiments, or decision criteria for the scope;
- factual support, attribution, and honest author context;
- jargon explained at the point of need;
- purposeful links, volatile details, and duplicate or drifting guidance.

Report findings as:

`severity | path | evidence | impact | remediation | validation`

Use `blocker` for a concrete correctness or broken-route problem, `warning`
for a material clarity, evidence, or maintenance risk, and `note` for a bounded
improvement. Do not invent a source or personal perspective. For a review-only
request, report findings without changing the page.
